<strong>Question 1</strong> <br>
Evaluate $\frac{a^3+b^3}{c^2+d^2}$ where $a=2$, $b=-2$, $c=4$, and $d=-2$. <br> 
(A) $\frac{4}{5}$ <br>
(B) $\frac{4}{3}$ <br>
(C) $\frac{3}{4}$ <br>
(D) $\frac{5}{4}$ <br>
(E) $0$ <br>
(F) None of the above

<details>
  <summary><strong>Solution</strong></summary>
  $\frac{a^3+b^3}{c^2+d^2}$ <br>
  $=\frac{(2)^3+(-2)^3}{(4)^2+(-2)^2}$ <br>
  $=\frac{8-8}{16+4}$ <br>
  $=\frac{0}{20}$ <br>
  $=0$ <br>
  $\therefore{E}$
</details>
