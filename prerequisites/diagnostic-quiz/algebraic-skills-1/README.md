# Algebraic Skills I

## Questions

1. Evaluate $\frac{a^3+b^3}{c^2+d^2}$ where $a=2$, $b=-2$, $c=4$, and $d=-2$. <br> 
(A) $\frac{4}{5}$ <br>
(B) $\frac{4}{3}$ <br>
(C) $\frac{3}{4}$ <br>
(D) $\frac{5}{4}$ <br>
(E) $0$ <br>
(F) None of the above

2. Simplify $\frac{x^8x^{-2}}{x^{-6}x^2}$. <br>
(A) $x^2$
(B) $x^14$
(C) $x^{-2}$
(D) $x^{-14}$
(E) $x^{-10}$
(F) None of the above

## Solutions

1. 

```math
\begin{aligned}
  \frac{a^3+b^3}{c^2+d^2} &= \frac{2^3 + (-2)^3}{4^2 + (-2)^2} \\
  &= \frac{8 - 8}{16 + 4} \\
  &= \frac{0}{20} \\
  &= 0 \\
  \therefore{E}
\end{aligned}
```

2.

```math
\begin{aligned}
  \frac{x^8x^{-2}}{x^{-6}x^2} &= \frac{x^{8-2}}{x^{-6+2}} \\
  &= \frac{x^6}{x^{-4}} \\
  &= x^{6-(-4)} \\
  &= x^{6+4} \\
  &= x^{10} \\
  \therefore{F}
\end{aligned}
```
